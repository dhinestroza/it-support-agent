# Password reset

## When this applies
Use this guide when an employee cannot log in because they forgot their
password, their password expired, or their account got locked after
multiple failed attempts.

## Self-service reset
Employees can reset their own password at any time by going to
`https://accounts.internal.company.com/reset`, entering their work email,
and following the link sent to their recovery email. The link expires
after 15 minutes.

## Locked account
After 5 failed login attempts, the account locks automatically for 30
minutes. The employee can wait it out, or use the self-service reset
above to unlock it immediately by setting a new password.

## When to escalate instead of resolving directly
Escalate to IT if: the employee doesn't have access to their recovery
email either, the account shows signs of suspicious activity (logins from
unrecognized locations), or the reset link isn't arriving after 10+
minutes and spam folder was already checked.
